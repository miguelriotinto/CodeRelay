# Code[Relay] Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Code[Relay] (the iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Android and Linux apps) is a client for
the CodeRelay server, which you install and run on your own computer. This
policy explains what the apps do with your data.

## Summary

**The developer collects no data.** The apps have no analytics, no tracking,
no advertising and no crash reporting, and they do not talk to any server run
by the developer. There is no account to create.

## What stays on your device

- **Server bookmarks** (host name, port, display name) and app preferences are
  stored on your device.
- **Access tokens** for your servers are stored in the system keychain
  (Keychain on Apple platforms, Android Keystore-backed storage, or the Secret
  Service on Linux).

## What goes to your own server

Everything you type in a terminal session, the terminal output, images you
paste into a session and clipboard text copied in a session travel directly
between the app and the CodeRelay server **you** configured. That server runs
on your computer, under your control; the developer has no access to it.

## Push notifications (optional)

If you turn on notifications, the app sends a push token to **your** server
so it can tell you when a coding agent finishes or needs input. Notifications
are delivered by Apple Push Notification service (Apple platforms) or Firebase
Cloud Messaging (Android), under Apple's and Google's privacy policies. The
notification text is generated by your server.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan a pairing QR code shown by your server. Images
are processed on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

## Children

The apps are developer tools and are not directed at children.
